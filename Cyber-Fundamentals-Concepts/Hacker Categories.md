# **Cyber 101: Who Are the Hackers?**

**Cyber Fundamentals**

## **What is a Hacker?**

A **hacker** is an individual who uses technical skills to identify and exploit vulnerabilities in systems, networks, or software.
Hacking can be **illegal or legal**, depending on **intent** and **authorization**.

* **Malicious intent** → illegal hacking
* **Defensive / authorized intent** → ethical hacking

---

## **Types of Hackers**

### **1) Criminal Hackers**

* Motivated by **financial gain**
* Target individuals and organizations
* Activities include:

  * Stealing credit card data
  * Compromising email or banking accounts
* **Example:** Albert Gonzalez (massive credit card theft)

---

### **2) Nation-State (State-Sponsored) Hackers**

* Backed by **governments**
* Highly sophisticated
* Target:

  * Other governments
  * Critical infrastructure
  * Large corporations
* Used for **espionage, surveillance, or political control**
* **Example:** Salt Typhoon (PRC-linked cyber espionage group)

---

### **3) Hacktivists**

* Hacking driven by **political, social, or ideological causes**
* Target governments, corporations, or individuals
* Aim to **promote an agenda**, not necessarily financial gain
* **Example:** Anonymous

---

### **4) Cyberterrorists**

* Conduct attacks to cause:

  * **Fear**
  * **Large-scale disruption**
  * **Physical harm or loss of life**
* More extreme than hacktivists
* Focused on **terror and catastrophic impact**

---

### **5) Insider Threats**

* Employees with **legitimate access**
* Abuse **trust**, not technical vulnerabilities
* Can be:

  * Malicious insiders
  * Negligent insiders
  * Colluding with external attackers
* **Example:** Roger Duronio (logic bomb at UBS)

---

### **6) Lone Wolf Hackers**

* Operate **independently**
* Harder to detect or predict
* No group structure
* **Example:** Jonathan James (hacked U.S. Defense systems)

---

### **7) Script Kiddies**

* Low skill level
* Use **pre-made tools and scripts**
* Often unaware of consequences
* Can cause **accidental large-scale damage**
* **Example:** Lizard Squad (DDoS attacks)

---

### **8) Ethical Hackers**

* Authorized cybersecurity professionals
* Identify and fix vulnerabilities
* Work with permission
* Often participate in **bug bounty programs**
* **Example:** Charlie Miller (Apple & automotive security research)

---

## **Key Takeaways**

* Hackers are categorized by **intent, skill level, and authorization**
* Not all hackers are criminals
* Ethical hackers play a **critical role in defense**
* Insider threats are especially dangerous due to **trusted access**

---

## **Exam Tip**

If the question mentions:

* **Money theft** → Criminal hacker
* **Government-backed espionage** → Nation-state hacker
* **Political or social causes** → Hacktivist
* **Fear / physical harm** → Cyberterrorist
* **Employee misuse** → Insider threat
* **Working alone** → Lone wolf
* **Using tools without skill** → Script kiddie
* **Authorized security testing** → Ethical hacker
